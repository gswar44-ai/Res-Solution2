ℹ️ About Res. Solution

Making Society Maintenance Simple

🏢 Who We Are

Res. Solution is a residential maintenance management platform designed to make communication between residents and society management easier.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a simple and organized way for residents to report problems and help secretaries manage complaints efficiently.

⚙️ How It Works

🏠 Residents submit maintenance issues.

🧑‍💼 Secretaries review and manage complaints.

🔧 Problems are resolved through proper coordination.

🚀 Future Vision

Res. Solution aims to become a smarter platform with features like AI assistance, notifications, faster complaint resolution and more.
